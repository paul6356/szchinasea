About Us

SHENZHEN CHINA SEA INTERNATIONAL LOGISTICS LIMITED

Shenzhen Zhongyihai International Freight Forwarding Co., Ltd., established in 2016, is a Class A freight forwarder registered with the Ministry of Commerce of China and a non-vessel operating common carrier (NVOCC) registered with the Ministry of Transport of China (MOC-NV 09778). It is also a member of the largest international freight forwarding organization alliance, WCA-GAA (ID: 110279), and has established a comprehensive global agent network. Zhongyihai is located at 105-B, 1st Floor, No. 1, Puxia Industrial Zone, Puxia Road, Liuyue Community, Henggang Town, Longgang District, Shenzhen.
,We are a diversified service company specializing in international air and sea freight, dangerous goods transportation, warehousing, customs clearance, inspection, and insurance. With decades of experience in international freight transportation, we have built a professional team dedicated to providing high-quality services to our clients. We have partnered with many internationally renowned brands, such as TCL Refrigeration Equipment Co., Ltd., Midea Air Conditioning, and Siemens Co., Ltd.
